1. Clarity of concept and positioning • Define core services: o Admission counselling: College/course selection, form filling, documentation, scholarship guidance. o Placements: CV building, interview prep, connecting students to employers, campus drives. o Trainings & courses: Short-term skill programs (soft skills, English, computer, pharma skills, etc.). • Decide target segment: o School students: Class 9–12, JEECUP, NEET, JEE, CUET, etc. o College students: D.Pharm, B.Pharm, B.Tech, BBA, etc. o Job seekers: Freshers, local graduates, paramedical, pharma sales, etc. • Positioning line (example): o “Complete Career Support: Admissions, Skills & Placements for Students of Western UP & Uttarakhand.” 2. Legal and business setup (India context) • Business form: o Sole proprietorship (fastest) or Partnership/LLP/Private Ltd if you plan to scale big. • Basic registrations: o PAN (personal or business). o Current account in bank (in agency name). o GST when annual turnover likely crosses ₹20 lakh; till then you can start without GST but keep records. • Name protection: o Apply for Trademark (Class 35 & 41) for your agency name (like “Vibes Admissions & Placements”). • Optional but wise: o Professional indemnity insurance (protects against claims due to wrong advice). o Simple service agreement / terms & conditions for students/parents. 3. Service design and fee structure Think in packages and heads so you can bill clearly and avoid confusion. 3.1 Admission counselling • Service heads: o Profile assessment: Academic record, interests, budget, location. o Course & college mapping: Shortlist of options (Govt/Private, fees, cutoffs). o Form filling & documentation: Online forms, document upload, corrections. o Choice filling & locking: For counselling portals (JEECUP, CUET, state counselling). o Follow-up & seat confirmation: Tracking allotment, reporting, fee payment guidance. • Fee models (per student): o Basic package (Form + guidance): ₹500–₹1,500 o Standard package (end-to-end counselling): ₹2,000–₹5,000 o Premium (multiple exams + full season support): ₹6,000–₹10,000+ • Other options: o Per form fee: ₹100–₹300 per form (for mass students). o Institution tie-up: College/school pays you per admitted student (e.g., ₹500–₹2,000 per conversion). 3.2 Placements • Service heads: o CV & LinkedIn profile building. o Interview training & mock interviews. o Job search support: Sharing openings, referrals, connecting to HR. o Placement drives: Organizing campus or pooled drives. • Fee models: o Student-side fee: ▪ CV + interview package: ₹500–₹1,500 ▪ Full placement support (till first job): ₹2,000–₹5,000 o Employer-side fee: ▪ Per candidate hired: 5–10% of first month salary or fixed ₹3,000₹10,000 per hire. o Institution-side fee: ▪ Annual retainer for placement cell support: ₹30,000–₹2,00,000 per year depending on size. 3.3 Trainings and courses • Types of trainings: o Soft skills: Communication, interview skills, personality development. o Technical skills: Pharma sales, hospital documentation, computer basics, Excel, Tally, etc. o Entrance support: Crash courses for JEECUP, CUET, etc. • Fee models: o Short course (10–20 hours): ₹1,000–₹3,000 per student. o Certificate course (30–60 hours): ₹3,000–₹10,000 per student. o Institution batch: Per batch fee (e.g., ₹20,000–₹1,00,000 for 30–100 students). • Cost heads: o Trainer honorarium, content, printing, hall/online platform, marketing, certificates. 3.4 Other revenue heads • Assessment fees: o Psychometric tests, aptitude tests: ₹500–₹2,000 per test. • Workshops/seminars: o Per student: ₹50–₹200 (large school seminars). o Per institution: lump sum ₹5,000–₹50,000 per event. • Online products: o Recorded courses, e-books, templates (forms, CV formats, etc.). 4. Operational SOP (step-by-step) 4.1 Pre-launch setup • Branding: o Name, logo, tagline, colour theme. o Basic website or landing page + Google Business profile. • Office & infrastructure: o Small office or cabin near schools/colleges/coaching hubs. o Laptop, printer, scanner, internet, phone/WhatsApp Business. • Documentation: o Service brochures, fee charts, consent forms, privacy note. o Student registration form (Google Form + physical). 4.2 Student onboarding process 1. Enquiry capture: o Walk-in, WhatsApp, phone, social media, school seminar leads. o Record in Excel/CRM: Name, class, contact, interest, source. 2. Initial counselling (free or low-cost): o 15–30 min session to understand needs. o Explain services, packages, and expected outcomes. 3. Proposal & fee confirmation: o Show clear package options and written fee (WhatsApp PDF or brochure). o Mention what is included and excluded. 4. Registration & payment: o Fill registration form, collect documents. o Take payment (cash/UPI/bank) and issue receipt. 5. Service delivery: o Admission: timeline of forms, counselling dates, follow-up. o Placement: schedule sessions, share job leads, track progress. o Training: batch schedule, attendance, assignments. 6. Closure & feedback: o Confirm outcome (admission/offer letter/course completion). o Take feedback and testimonial (photo + quote). o Ask for referrals. 4.3 Daily operations • Morning: o Check messages, update student tracking sheet, plan sessions. • Daytime: o Counselling sessions, training classes, calls with HR/colleges. • Evening: o Follow-up messages, next-day schedule, social media posts. • Weekly: o Review leads, conversions, revenue, pending cases. • Monthly: o Analyze which services are working, adjust fees and marketing. 5. Marketing and student acquisition 5.1 Low-cost, high-impact channels • WhatsApp: o Broadcast lists for students, parents, teachers. o Admission alerts, last date reminders, success stories. • School & college tie-ups: o Offer free seminars: “Career Options after 12th”, “Pharmacy Careers”, etc. o Collect leads via Google Form or paper slips. • Local coaching centres: o Partner for counselling days; share revenue or pay per lead. • Posters & flex boards: o Near schools, coaching centres, cyber cafés, bus stands. 5.2 Digital presence • Google Business profile: o Location, photos, reviews, contact. • Social media (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube): o Short videos explaining admissions, cut-offs, career paths. o Student success reels and testimonials. • Referral system: o Give small discount or bonus session for each successful referral. 6. Quality, ethics and compliance • Transparency in fees: o Always give written fee structure; avoid hidden charges. • No false promises: o Never guarantee admission/placement where you don’t control the decision. • Data protection: o Take consent for storing data and using photos. o Keep student records secure (password-protected files). • Continuous learning: o Stay updated on new exams, counselling rules, job trends. o Attend webinars, connect with other counsellors and HRs. 7. Financial planning (basic) • Fixed costs: o Rent, electricity, internet, staff salary, software subscriptions. • Variable costs: o Printing, travel, seminar expenses, trainer payments. • Pricing logic: o Target gross margin of at least 40–60% per service. o Example: If a training batch costs you ₹20,000 total, charge ₹35,000₹50,000+. • Record keeping: o Maintain simple books: date-wise income, expense, profit. o Use Excel or basic accounting software. 8. From scratch to “full operations” roadmap • Month 1–2: o Register business, set up office, design services & fees, create basic marketing materials. • Month 3–4: o Start seminars in nearby schools/colleges, run first training batch, handle initial admissions season. • Month 5–6: o Formalize placement network (HR contacts), refine packages, collect testimonials. • Month 7–12: o Scale: more institutions, online sessions (Zoom/Meet), recorded courses, bigger batches.
